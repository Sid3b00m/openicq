OpenICQ is an independent, clean-room reimplementation inspired by the ICQ
instant messenger. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from ICQ,
AOL, Mirabilis, or VK.

No original ICQ code, artwork, or audio is included. The status flowers and
other icons are drawn in SVG by hand, and every sound is synthesized at runtime
with the Web Audio API.
